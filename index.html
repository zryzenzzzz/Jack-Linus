FAQ

What areas do you deliver to?
Free delivery within 30 miles of our yard; small mileage fee after that. We set your shed on concrete blocks or your prepared pad.
What foundation do I need?
Level ground is essential. We recommend a compacted gravel pad or concrete. We can level within 4" using pressure‑treated shims.
What’s the lead time?
Typical lead time is 2–3 weeks. Installation takes about one day depending on size and site access.
Do you offer financing?
Yes — with approved credit, payments can be as low as $64/mo on smaller models. Ask us for details.
